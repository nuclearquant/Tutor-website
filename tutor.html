Where this data lives

Read this before you rely on it. Right now the register is saved inside this browser on this device. It survives closing the tab and restarting the laptop, but it does not travel: your phone, and a student's phone, each hold their own separate copy. To have one shared register that everyone sees, follow the Supabase steps in README-dashboards.md — it is a free account and about twenty minutes of work, and no screen in these dashboards has to change.

Microsoft Teams

What works now. Paste a Teams join link into a lesson row and the student gets a Join button and a calendar file that puts the lesson in their Teams and Outlook calendar with a reminder. Accept, Decline and Pending are recorded here in the register when the student taps them.

What needs a server. Having Teams itself create the meeting, and reading a student's real Teams RSVP back automatically, goes through the Microsoft Graph API. That needs a secret key that cannot be put in a web page. README-dashboards.md sets out that route.
